Photography Tips

  • Use daylight. Avoid flash and hard shadows across the leaf.
  • Get close. Fill the frame with one leaf or a small group of plants.
  • Keep it sharp. Hold the phone steady so the leaf veins are clear.
  • One crop per photo. Do not mix rice and maize in the same picture.
